

# [`⍹`](https://answerdotai.github.io/basedpl/glyphs/omega-underbar.html) — Right operand

Argument. Keys: `Alt-Minus w`. Hanzi: `丁`

In a dfn,
[`⍹`](https://answerdotai.github.io/basedpl/glyphs/omega-underbar.html)
is the right operand. A dfn that uses
[`⍹`](https://answerdotai.github.io/basedpl/glyphs/omega-underbar.html)
is a dyadic operator.

``` bpl
op←{⍶+⍹×ω} ◇ (2 op 3)4 ⍝ 14
```

Operands can be subjects or functions. The arguments are still
[`α`](https://answerdotai.github.io/basedpl/glyphs/alpha.html) and
[`ω`](https://answerdotai.github.io/basedpl/glyphs/omega.html).
