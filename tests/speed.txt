# The cases that `tests/speed.rs` times in the native build and `tests/wasm-speed.mjs` times in the browser build. Each case line holds the code, two native budgets and a wasm budget, separated by tabs. The first native budget is for macOS on Apple silicon, where matrix products and decompositions run on Accelerate. The second is for Linux on CI's x86 runner, where they run on rustymath's own kernels. `rustymath::timing::NATIVE_COLUMN` picks the native budget a machine checks. Each section starts with its name in brackets and the prefs its session starts with. Every session then runs the lines under the `[setup]` before its section.
# Budgets in `[copies]`, `[exact]`, `[images]`, `[objectives]`, `[calls]`, `[derivatives]`, `[structure]` and `[maps]` count copies of the `n` items that the section's setup makes. A budget in `[widths]` is the most that the time in that section's 32-bit session may be, as a fraction of the time in the `[exact]` session.
# An initial budget in copies is twice what a first-principles analysis says the case should cost, and at least 0.5. An initial `[widths]` budget is 0.25 more than the fraction that such an analysis expects. A later budget comes from a fuller understanding of the case than that analysis gives, such as a platform difference or a cost the analysis missed. A comment above each case, or above its group, gives the expected cost and its reason, as in "1 copy: one read and one write per item", and records each later change with its reason. `basedpl.bpltests.set_budgets` writes budgets from expected costs.
# One expected cost serves every platform at first. A platform's budget differs only where a specific difference on that platform is found.
# A case that costs more than its budget needs study, which finds either a speed bug to fix or something the analysis missed. Avoid raising a budget to match a run without understanding why the case costs what it does.
# A line above a case can name a slow-path event that `•explain` records for it, named as its record names it, with any `"` doubled: `# allows RECORD "EVENT": principle` where a principle allows the event, and `# gap RECORD "EVENT": what should happen` where a faster route should remove it. `tests/explain.rs` fails on any other slow-path event, and on a line whose event no longer appears.
