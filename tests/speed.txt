Every session then runs the lines under the `[setup]` before its section.
# Budgets in `[copies]`, `[exact]`, `[images]` and `[objectives]` count copies of the `n` items that the section's setup makes. A budget in `[widths]` is the most that the time in that section's 32-bit session may be, as a fraction of the time in the `[exact]` session. Natively and in wasm, it is the worse of that fraction on a Mac and on CI's runner, plus 0.25.
