# The cases that `tests/speed.rs` times in the native build and `tests/wasm-speed.mjs` times in the browser build. Each case line holds the code, its native budget and its wasm budget, separated by tabs. Each section starts with its name in brackets and the prefs its session starts with. Every session then runs the lines under the `[setup]` before its section.
# Budgets in `[copies]`, `[exact]` and `[images]` count copies of the `n` items that the section's setup makes. A budget in `[widths]` is the most that the time in that section's 32-bit session may be, as a fraction of the time in the `[exact]` session. Natively and in wasm, it is the worse of that fraction on a Mac and on CI's runner, plus 0.25.
# A wasm budget in copies is twice the most the case has cost on a Mac and on CI's runner, in the module that `cargo wasm` builds, and at least 0.5. `tests/speed.rs` gives the same rule for native budgets in copies.
# A case that CI hasn't timed yet has four times its Mac cost, natively and in wasm, until the next `Speed` run sets its budgets under these rules.
